The force

Exactly 4 to 8 digits; leading zeros are preserved. With "None", only the exact force number works. A time suffix, when enabled, is part of the required code.

For a live suffix, the time is captured on the first digit and held for that attempt, for up to 30 seconds. A new attempt uses the new time.

Appearance

Uses the iPhone's own system font on iOS. Clock sizes are adjustable because iOS versions, devices and custom Lock Screens differ. Automatic avoids drawing a second status bar in an installed iOS web app.

After unlocking

For the closest match, upload an unedited, full-screen screenshot from this same iPhone. It includes your real wallpaper, app icons, labels and dock. Screenshots are visual only: they cannot launch your installed apps. Two screenshots can be swiped between. Their captured clock stays static.

Controls: Tap the clock or swipe up for the keypad. Hold the clock, small passcode lock, or empty Home Screen background for settings. Double-tap empty Home Screen background to relock. On a keyboard: digits, Backspace, Escape; S opens settings and L relocks.

iPhone setup: Open the hosted HTML in Safari, use Share > Add to Home Screen, enable Open as Web App when offered, and launch its icon. File previews may not execute JavaScript. This single file has no external dependencies, but guaranteed offline relaunch would require hosting plus a service worker.

A local visual prop, not device security. Do not enter a real iPhone passcode. No typed entries are saved or transmitted. The chosen force and appearance settings are stored locally when storage is available; a configured export also contains them. HTML cannot unlock iOS or automatically return to the actual system Home Screen. Use the iPhone's Home gesture to leave.
